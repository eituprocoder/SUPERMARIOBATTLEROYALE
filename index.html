HOW TO PLAY?

Be the last survivor — or the last team standing.

MOVEMENT & CAMERAMove with WASD / arrows or the joystick. Drag the arena to rotate the camera. Movement follows the camera direction, while throwable items follow the direction your character is facing.
ENERGYEvery fighter starts with 3 energy. Super Mushrooms restore 1 energy, up to 3.
FALL ELIMINATIONPlayers can be knocked beyond the arena edge. Falling off eliminates the player immediately, regardless of remaining energy.
STOMP & TRIPLE JUMPJump on opponents to damage them. Chain 3 well-timed jumps: the third jump is higher and its stomp deals 2 energy.
GROUND POUNDPress JUMP again while airborne. You slam downward. Players directly under the impact lose 2 energy; nearby enemies are blasted away.
GREEN SHELLDefeat a Koopa to make it drop a Green Shell. Press USE ITEM to throw it in the direction your character is facing. It bounces from arena obstacles, then knocks down the first enemy player it hits.
FIRE FLOWERGrants 5 fireballs. Each fireball bounces once, knocks the target back, briefly stuns them and removes 1 energy.
GOOMBAS & KOOPASEnemies roam the battlefield. Stomp them to defeat them. Side contact can hurt you.
DUOSTeams of 2. Teammates cannot hurt or knock back one another. Your duo wins when every other team is eliminated.
SQUADSTwo teams, up to 5 players per team. Friendly fire is disabled. The surviving team wins.
SUDDEN DEATHWhen only 2 rival players remain, a 30-second timer begins. If neither wins before it reaches 0, both are defeated and the match ends in a draw.
PLACEMENTSThe first player eliminated receives the lowest placement (#10 in a full match), then placements rise with each elimination until #1.
